How these reports work

  • The source is the board’s own record. Every decision links to the page of the official minutes on hsd.ca where it appears. News coverage fills in what the minutes leave out, attributed and linked.
  • Trustees are named; residents are not. Trustees and senior division staff are named. Parents, students and other members of the public are identified by initials only, or not at all.
  • What the record can’t show, we say. The minutes record vote counts, not how each trustee voted, and HSD does not post meeting video. Each report has an “About this page” section saying what could not be confirmed.
