The first reports are being written

We are working through every public board meeting since July 2013: 158 sets of minutes and 1,660 motions, plus the agendas, budgets, audited statements and division policies. Reports will appear here by topic.

How these reports will work

  • The source is the board's own record. Every decision links to the page of the official minutes on hsd.ca where it appears.
  • Trustees are named; residents are not. Trustees and division staff are named. Members of the public are identified by initials only.
  • What the record can't show, we say. The minutes record vote counts, not how each trustee voted, and HSD does not post meeting video. Each report has an “About this page” section saying what could not be confirmed.
