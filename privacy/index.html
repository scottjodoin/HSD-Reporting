Privacy

In short

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, and we can't see those logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

People named in reports

Trustees and division staff are named, because they act in public roles. Members of the public who appear in the board's records are identified by initials only.
